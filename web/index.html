DeepWeeds classifier

Upload a top-down photo of ground vegetation. The model answers with one of eight weed species, or "Negative" when no target weed is present. Inference runs entirely in your browser (ONNX Runtime Web); the image is not uploaded anywhere.

Model: MobileNetV3-Large fine-tuned on DeepWeeds, test macro-F1 0.81 (see the project README for the
evaluation and its limits). Sample images are from the DeepWeeds dataset (Olsen et al., 2019, CC BY 4.0).
"Low confidence" means the calibrated confidence is under the threshold chosen on validation data.
